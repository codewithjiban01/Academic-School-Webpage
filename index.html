About Our Academy

18 years of educational excellence in Dang, District

Our Heritage

Founded in 2007, Purandhara Everest Academy has established itself as a beacon of quality education in Western, Dang. We combine modern teaching methodologies with traditional values to create well-rounded individuals prepared for global challenges.

Academic Distinction

Consistent 100% pass rate with 92% distinction in SEE examinations, with our students regularly securing top rankings.

Holistic Development

20+ extracurricular clubs and annual participation in national/international competitions across academics, sports, and arts.

Our Mission

To ignite intellectual curiosity, foster creativity, and develop ethical leaders through innovative education in a nurturing environment.

Our Vision

To be Nepal's premier educational institution that transforms students into globally competent, socially responsible citizens.
